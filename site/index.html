03

Make it yours

Adjust your player settings, follow installation progress and launch an installed game without signing in again.

Get started

Choose a folder. Install. Play.

Download and extract the package for your system, open QuakeDock, and choose Campaign or Multiplayer. Select a game folder and sign in to a Steam account that owns Quake. Once installation finishes, press Play. No Python installation needed.
